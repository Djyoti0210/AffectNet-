--- CRITICAL ANALYSIS ---
The performance dropped across all minority classes. Why? 
Because of a mathematical sign inversion in Menon's Logit Adjustment formulation between Inference vs Training.

At INFERENCE time, the formula to unbias the network is:
    prediction = argmax( logits - tau * log(p) )
We subtract the negative log frequency to artificially BOOST the minority logits so they can win the argmax.

But during TRAINING, if we boost the minority logits BEFORE applying Cross Entropy, we are artificially lowering the loss for minority samples. The network thinks it's already doing a great job on Contempt/Disgust, so the gradients vanish!

To properly bake Logit Adjustment into the TRAINING loss (often called LA-Loss), the sign must be inverted:
    adjusted_logits = logits + tau * log(p)
This severely PENALIZES the minority logits before CE is calculated, creating a massive loss spike for minority classes and forcing the optimizer to push their weights much harder.
